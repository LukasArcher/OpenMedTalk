# Governance (initial proposal)
Maintainer owns official release decisions. Project goal: free official software, no advertising or clinical paywall. Contributors may propose code, content and UI changes; clinical source review is separate from software review. See docs/CLINICAL.md for independent reviewer and community consensus paths, risk policy and withdrawal. Nobody approves their own translation. Do not accept clinical material from third parties without permission or copy competitors' phrase databases. Contributor credit opt-in only.
The existing MPL-2.0 license remains in place. Proposed future split: mobile and public web MPL-2.0; translation portal AGPL-3.0; independently licensed question and translation data CC BY-SA 4.0; docs CC BY 4.0; name/logo reserved subject to legal review. Maintainer must explicitly confirm before separate licenses are added; review compatibility for shared packages and establish contribution terms. No guarantee that commercial forks are prohibited by open source licenses.
Protect the default branch and release process when collaborators join; enforce CI and independent clinical approval. Name/trademark clearance and regulatory review remain open.
